## Description

Sawadeeka is a two-way Chinese–Thai learning app. Choose your direction, then learn how the same idea changes across formal, polite, casual and high-risk situations.

The app includes 3,000 training cards, Chinese/Thai text, romanisation, pronunciation memory cues, tap-to-hear examples, recording playback, speaking quests, speed-based monster battles and same-device duels. Progress, favourites, mistakes and scores stay on your device and can be exported or cleared.

S5–S1 rates the social risk of an expression, not the learner. S1 contains strong or confrontational language only for recognition and safe-response practice; it is not available as a repeat-after-me exercise.

The store build works offline for bundled lessons and audio. It has no account, ads or behavioural analytics. Microphone access is requested only after you start a speaking activity. Optional online speech recognition is attempted only with explicit permission and depends on device support.

Pronunciation scores are practice feedback, not accent certification or a professional examination result.
