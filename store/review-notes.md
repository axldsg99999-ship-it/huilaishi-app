## 中文

萨瓦迪卡是无需账号的中泰双向语言学习应用。审核员无需登录或演示账号。

首次启动：

1. 选择“中国人学泰语”或“泰国人学中文”。
2. 默认建议 S4 日常礼貌档位；进入后可从底部导航打开课程、词库、游戏与设置。
3. 麦克风只在点击跟读、发音闯关或录音按钮后请求。拒绝权限不会阻止文字课程和选择题。

需要特别说明：

- S5–S1 是表达场景风险，不是用户等级。S1 含强烈语言，只用于识别；进入前显示风险确认，不提供跟读按钮，并提供 S4 安全回应。
- 发音分数是设备能力范围内的练习反馈，不宣称口音认证。
- App Store / Google Play 构建不包含网页测试版的真人 WebRTC 邀请房，没有聊天、随机匹配或用户生成内容。
- 没有账号、广告、分析 SDK、远程学习记录或应用内购买。
- 课程和内置语音离线可用；可选联网语音识别只在用户明确允许本次使用时尝试。
- 本机数据可在“设置 → 导出学习数据 / 清除本机学习数据”管理。

## English

Sawadeeka is a two-way Chinese–Thai learning app with no account or demo login.

On first launch, select Chinese-to-Thai or Thai-to-Chinese. S4 is the recommended everyday-polite register. Courses, vocabulary, games and settings are available from the main navigation. Microphone permission is requested only after the reviewer starts a speaking or recording activity; text lessons remain usable if permission is denied.

S5–S1 describes expression risk, not user status. S1 contains strong language for recognition only, has a warning gate, does not offer repeat-after-me controls and teaches a safer S4 response. Pronunciation scores are practice feedback, not accent certification.

The App Store / Google Play binary excludes the human WebRTC invitation room available in the separate web beta. The store build has no chat, random matching or user-generated content. It also has no account, ads, analytics SDK, remote learning history or in-app purchases. Bundled lessons/audio work offline. Optional online speech recognition is attempted only after explicit per-use permission.
